3. The pattern: it's decided by region and route, not by each person
- The driver starts from the south (London / Essex). Everyone in London and the South East is covered locally, even people who accept post. That's why "South" is the biggest group.
- "Up north" is one road trip up the east side, up the M1/A1: Birmingham and Leicester first, then Newcastle. Those regions have people who must be met in person (accepting post = No, "In person meet"): Drew, Hypz and Shumon in the Midlands, Fahim in Newcastle. Since the driver has to go anyway, everyone else there is dropped off on the same trip, even those who say Yes to post (Mayah, Geradas, Teddy, Shayne).
- "To be posted" is the regions off that route (Liverpool in the west, Glasgow far north-west) where nobody needs an in-person meet: every row there says Accepting postals = Yes. So a special trip isn't worth it, and the money is posted.
- The regional rule overrides the individual flag. Jim ("Handled internally") and Ryan ("Subject to change") are still in "posted" because their region is.
- In short: a region gets driven if it's local (south) or if anyone there needs an in-person meet on the northbound route. A region where everyone accepts post and that's off the route gets posted.
